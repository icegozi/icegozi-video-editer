---
name: icegozi-short-video
description: Plan and build a short explainer, TikTok, Reels or Shorts video from a topic, script and existing local media in ICEGOZI.
---

Use `get_project_context`, `list_project_assets` and `get_editor_capabilities`.
Preserve explicit duration, platform, aspect ratio and tone. For a requested
vertical short, use a 9:16 canvas if the relevant tracks are editable; otherwise
explain the locked setting. Keep the existing FPS unless instructed otherwise.

Turn the brief into an opening hook, a small number of clear beats, and a useful
ending. Allocate frame durations so the total matches the requested runtime,
including crossfade overlaps. Keep on-screen text short enough to read. Avoid
inventing facts, quotes, testimonials or media that the user did not supply.

Build scenes with supported text, image, video or formula fields. Use imported
audio when available and timed subtitle cues for supplied narration. Check
`generation` capabilities before promising new images, footage, voices or music;
if unavailable, create a useful text/media draft and identify what to import.

Validate the complete candidate and propose it for review. Render only after
the user applies the draft and asks for a preview or final output.
